Travel Plans

Ways to shape your time here

Three sketches rather than fixed itineraries — a one-night stop, a proper two-night stay, and a slower few days with the countryside included.

Home/Travel Plans

How to read these. The plans below describe the shape of a visit, not a timetable. Exact timings, transport, inclusions and prices depend on your dates and are confirmed with you when you enquire — nothing here is a quotation.

1Night

The overnight stop

Colombo ↔ Kandy

For travellers crossing the island who want to see the elephants properly instead of in a forty-minute coach stop.

Afternoon arrival

Check in, swim or sit by the river, and get your bearings before dinner at the hotel.

Early start

Out before the day visitors. The cool hour is when the elephants are liveliest and the light is best.

River and breakfast

The walk down to the Ma Oya and the bathe, then back for a late breakfast on the terrace.

On your way

Check out and continue to Kandy or back towards Colombo, having actually seen something.

2Nights

The proper visit

Most popular shape

Two nights gives you two mornings with the elephants and a whole day for everything else — which is when Pinnawala stops being a stop and becomes a place.

Day one — settle in

Arrive, unpack, and spend the late afternoon by the river or on your verandah. Dinner on site.

Day two — morning with the herd

Out early for feeding and the river. Back for breakfast and the hot part of the day in the shade.

Day two — afternoon out

A herbal garden walk, the village, or a drive into the Kegalle countryside. Back for the evening.

Day three — one more look

A second morning at the river before you check out, which is usually the one people remember.

3+Nights

The slow week

Both properties

Split your stay between the two: a couple of nights in the cabanas among the trees, then the hotel on the river — or the other way round.

The garden first

Two nights at Luck Uyana Cabana. Verandah mornings, lantern-lit evenings, nothing much to do.

Elephants at your own pace

With several days you can go out whenever conditions are good rather than when the schedule says.

A day further afield

Kandy and the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, or a drive through the estates and back roads.

Finish on the river

Move across to Pinnawala Reach for the last nights, with the restaurant and the water below your balcony.

Before you book

Things worth telling us

The more of this you include in your first message, the more useful our reply will be.

Your dates and how long

Even approximate dates help. Availability at both properties is limited — there are only eleven rooms and cabanas between them.

Who is travelling

Adults, children, ages, and whether you need rooms next to each other. It changes what we can offer.

How you are arriving

Own driver, hire car, train to Rambukkana or something else — so we can send the right directions and advise on transfers.
