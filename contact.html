Getting here

Finding us on the ground

Both properties are a short drive from Pinnawala village in Kegalle District, between Colombo and Kandy. Tell us how you are travelling and we will send directions that match your route.

By road

Off the Colombo–Kandy road near Kegalle. Both properties have parking on site.

By train

Rambukkana, on the main Colombo–Kandy line, is the nearest station. A short road transfer from there.

From the airport

Bandaranaike International Airport. Ask us about a transfer when you enquire.

Lost on the day?

Send us a WhatsApp message and we will talk you in, or send a tuk-tuk to meet you.
