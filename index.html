Welcome

Elephants, jungle and a warm Sri Lankan welcome

Pinnawala Elephant Tour brings together the elephant experiences of Pinnawala, two family-run places to stay and the quiet corners of Kegalle District that most visitors drive straight past.

Pinnawala sits in the low green hills between Colombo and Kandy, where the Ma Oya river runs through coconut groves and rubber estates. It is best known for its elephants — and it is one of the few places in the world where you can watch a large group of Asian elephants simply get on with an ordinary day.

We look after two places to stay here, both run by the same family: Pinnawala Reach Hotel, a seven-room hotel and restaurant perched above the river, and Luck Uyana Cabana, four timber cabanas raised on stilts in a jungle garden. One enquiry reaches both.

Where you stay

Two very different ways to sleep in the jungle

Both properties belong to the same family. Choose the riverside hotel, the elevated garden cabanas — or ask us which would suit your trip.

Pinnawala Reach Hotel

7 luxury rooms

A hotel and restaurant built right on the bank of the Ma Oya, where the dining terrace hangs over the water and the jungle starts on the far side.

Rooms are generous and cool, with air conditioning, a television, a private bathroom and a balcony or terrace that looks into the trees. Downstairs there is a reception lounge, an à la carte restaurant and a large dining hall used for buffets and functions.

Luck Uyana Cabana

4 luxury cabanas

Four timber cabanas lifted on stilts in a garden of fruit trees and flowering creepers, each one hand-painted with murals of elephants, lakes and village life.

Every cabana has its own verandah with jungle on three sides, an air-conditioned bedroom and a private bathroom. Meals are served in an open thatched pavilion under the trees, and the whole garden is strung with lanterns after dark.

Getting here

Pinnawala is easy to reach — and easy to miss

It sits roughly halfway along the main road between Colombo and Kandy, which makes it a natural stop on almost any Sri Lankan itinerary.

By road

Pinnawala lies off the Colombo–Kandy road near Kegalle. Most visitors arrive by private car or tuk-tuk. Tell us your route and we will send directions and advise on parking.

By train

Rambukkana is the nearest railway station, on the main Colombo–Kandy line. It is a short road transfer from there to both properties.

From the airport

Bandaranaike International Airport is the usual arrival point. Ask us when you enquire and we will help you arrange a transfer.

How long to stay

One night is enough to see the elephants without rushing. Two lets you take the river, the garden and the surrounding countryside at a gentler pace.
