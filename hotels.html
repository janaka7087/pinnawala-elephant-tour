Pinnawala Reach Hotel

Hotel & restaurant

Built on the bank of the Ma Oya, with the dining terrace cantilevered out over the water and jungle rising on the far side.

The hotel is arranged over two floors around a timber-framed entrance portico. Inside there is a reception lounge, an à la carte restaurant and a large dining hall used for buffets and family functions. Outside, a covered walkway follows the riverbank to a series of open terraces where breakfast is served.

The seven rooms are generous and cool. Each has air conditioning, a television, a desk, a private bathroom and either a balcony or a terrace looking into the trees — in several rooms, straight down at the river.

Luck Uyana Cabana

Garden cabanas

Four timber cabanas lifted on stilts in a garden of fruit trees and flowering creepers in Randeniya, on the Kegalle side of Pinnawala.

Each cabana is reached by its own staircase and has a private verandah with jungle on three sides. The buildings are painted in warm ochre and hung with hand-painted murals — elephants at a waterhole, lake scenes, village figures — so that the whole place feels made rather than built.

Bedrooms are air-conditioned and panelled in timber, with a private bathroom. Meals are taken in an open thatched pavilion under the trees, and after dark the paths and murals are lit with strings of warm lanterns.
